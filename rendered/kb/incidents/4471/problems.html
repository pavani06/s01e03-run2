Counts come from the desk's own rule in src/lib/kb/causes.ts and its notes in src/lib/data/desk-notes.json, over the export of 4 October, built when the page renders.

parser

The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for.

hint

A different bug with no ticket of its own. Tenants on the booking-v3 form show MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so their people type the month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Fixing the parser does not fix these: the hint is wrong, not the parser.

one-day move

Some reports are not a day and month swap at all: the date lands one day off, as in “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk read them as a one-day move and still files them under the parser by tenant, so they are counted there too. The parser fix will not move them back a day.

How to check

  • The cause of a report is causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts, never the intake tag, which guesses from the reporter’s browser.
  • The export repeats some reports across overlapping pages; each id counts once (uniqueById).
  • Each ticket lists the ids of the reports it covers.
